I.

Il Programma The Programme

Andante sospettoso, ma non troppo

Roma, 1975 · Giallo in un atto

A blind violinist. A black glove. One witness.

After years at the back of the string section, a blind violinist finally wins first chair of the symphony. Her triumph does not last the week.

One by one, her fellow musicians are being silenced by a killer in black leather gloves. The police have no leads. The orchestra is running out of oboists. And the only witness to the murders is the one member of the company who cannot testify: her seeing-eye dog.

Note sul restauro

NOTE SUL RESTAURO. In 1975 the director Enrico Januzzi began shooting a giallo in Rome. After one week the production collapsed. Accounts differ as to why: the payroll, the dog, the nobody will say.

The footage was presumed lost for fifty years. It resurfaced in 2025, and WBAZ Films has painstakingly restored the surviving fragment, which is presented here for the very first time.

    Il Testimone The Witness

    Deposizione, verbale n° 1975/14

    That is me, giving my statement. The helmet was supposed to help. It did not help.

    The witness answers only to her name. Type ZIGGY.

    Commissariato di Zona · Roma14 · III · 1975
    D: Il testimone dichiari le sue generalità.
    State your name for the record.
    R: Ziggy. Cane guida.
    D: Ha visto l'assassino?
    Did you see the killer?
    R: Bau.
    D: Era un uomo o una donna?
    Was it a man or a woman?
    R: bau bau bau bau.
    D: Di che colore erano i guanti?
    What colour were the gloves?
    R: [Il testimone indica un calzino nero.] [The witness points at a black sock.]
    D: Ha altro da aggiungere?
    Anything else to add?
    R: [Il testimone chiede un biscotto.]
